**Description about the following series**
: **All of the series that I provided is an anime series and each one of them has different genres.**

: *Ao Haru Ride (also known as Blue Spring Ride) is a coming-of-age shojo manga series written and illustrated by Io Sakisaka. The story follows high schooler Futaba Yoshioka as she reconnects with her middle school first love, Kou Tanaka (now Kou Mabuchi), who has become distant and guarded due to family and personal struggles.*

: *Hellsing Ultimate is a 10-episode action-horror anime OVA series (2006–2012) that follows the Hellsing Organization as it protects England from supernatural threats and a rogue neo-Nazi vampire army. Adapted from Kouta Hirano's manga, it features a three-way conflict between Hellsing, the Vatican's monster-hunting forces, and the Millennium group*

: *That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime (TenSura) follows 37-year-old Japanese salaryman Satoru Mikami, who is fatally stabbed on the street and reincarnates in a fantasy world as a powerful slime named Rimuru Tempest who builds a peaceful monster nation. Created by Fuse with illustrations by Mitz Vah, the series began as a web novel before expanding into light novels, manga, and anime adaptations*
